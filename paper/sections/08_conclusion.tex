\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Label-free self-play learns from problems the model is unsure about, but uncertainty has two sources and training resolves only one: solvable problems sharpen out of the admission band, which keeps problems whose text supports several readings, and sharpening makes the solver commit to one reading of some and decline others, unseen by benchmark accuracy. Uncertainty is not quality control: label-free self-play needs a measure of well-posedness independent of the solver's agreement, and its models a way to abstain.
