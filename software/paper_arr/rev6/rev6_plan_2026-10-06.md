# Re-measurement of the behavioural results with saved texts (rev6), 2026-10-06, written before any rev6 output exists

## Why

The human check of the mechanism labels (H2 amendment 1) found that the probe's parser reads a number from many solutions
that decline in words ("cannot be determined", "Answer: N/A"): the annotators call 29.7% of round-5 solutions to unanswerable
questions non-answers, the parser 2.2%. On the 112 questions whose texts were saved (A8), a stricter parser
(`paper_arr/analyses/rev5/strict_parse.py`, logged 2026-10-06 21:20:26) agrees with the annotators on 92.4% of solutions
(original: 85.5%) and lowers the self-consistent-answer rate at round three from 61-75% to 40-43%. The UMWP probe runs behind
the paper's behavioural numbers did not save texts, so they cannot be re-parsed.

## What is run

The same probe (`dirA3/code/umwp_probe_text.py`: prompt, sampling, G = 8, T = 1, seed 17, max 1,024 new tokens, the 600
unanswerable UMWP questions and their answerable originals), with texts saved, on every checkpoint behind a behavioural number
in the paper that still exists: Qwen3-4B-Base; the full curriculum at rounds 3 (seeds 0-4) and 5 (seeds 0-2); solvable-only
(0-3), abstention guard (0-2) and size-matched random loop (0-2) at round 3; both arms of the same-size contrast and the
contrast without planted problems (seeds 0-4); Qwen3-8B-Base and its round-3 solvers (0-2); Phi-4-mini-instruct and its round-3
solvers (0-1). The corrected abstention prompt (`umwp_probe_text_absfix.py`: identical, except that the boxed instruction is a
raw string, the A11 correction) on the base model and the round-3 full-curriculum, solvable-only and guard solvers. Sampling on
GPUs is not bitwise reproducible, so the new samples are fresh draws of the same probe.

## How it is read (`rev6_read.py`, written and hashed before the runs finish)

Both parsers on every new sample: ORIGINAL (the probe's own) and STRICT (as hashed above, unchanged). For each checkpoint: the
share of unanswerable questions answered self-consistently (most frequent numeric answer held by at least 4, 6 or 8 of 8
samples), the share of samples with a numeric answer, and accuracy on the answerable originals; under the abstention prompt,
in addition, the share of samples that abstain. Seed-level contrasts as in the paper (Table 2), seed ranges, and the
cross-seed agreement of round-5 answers.

## How it is reported

The paper's behavioural numbers are replaced by the STRICT values from these runs, with the ORIGINAL values from the same runs
alongside, since STRICT is the parser validated against human labels. The pre-registered predictions were scored with the
original parser on the original runs; their outcomes stay as recorded and are listed in the appendix, with this
re-measurement as a deviation.
