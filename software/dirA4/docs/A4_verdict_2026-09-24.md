## 2. What the registered reads establish

1. Drift. Agreement-band self-play curricula accumulate ill-posed questions in every lineage of four families (Qwen3-4B/8B,
   Phi-4-mini, Llama-3.2-3B through round 2) and in one Qwen3-14B round; post-training reproduces the shift (14B, 32B).
   It is judge-independent (A5 J-P2: on UMWP's own human labels the band's unanswerable/answerable in-band ratio rises
   1.4-4.5x in 10/10 powered lineages; J-P3 on the twins 8/10), and the judge agrees with UMWP's human labels at kappa 0.94
   (A5 J-P1) and with the authors' expert annotation at 0.77 / 0.69 [internal note removed].
2. Mechanism. Per-class sharpening law (beta_S 3.75 > beta_A 1.60 > beta_U 1.40; held-out MAE 2.4); label-free filters
   cannot stop the drift (NG1/NG3, PF1/PF2, PG2, PC3, exploratory guards).
3. Harm. Confident answering of unanswerable questions rises under self-play and has a drift-attributable component at round 3
   (HC1 4/4; HX1 4/4 on UMWP), invisible to benchmark accuracy (HC3). Limitation found by A5 F-P1/F-P3: this harm is measured
   with the plain prompt; when the abstention option is offered at test time, drift-trained and clean-trained models abstain
   about equally (UMWP ABS_U 67-70% for both), and a label-free test-time rule on the drifted own model reaches CA_U 0.19-0.23
   at the guard's false-abstention level.
4. Remedies. (a) The abstention guard removes the plain-prompt harm (PF4 3/3 + 2/2, HX5 3/3) at 0-3.7 points of accuracy, but it
   is dominated by test-time abstention prompting (A5 F-P1 fails 0/3 UMWP, 0/3 twins; F-P3 fails). (b) Offering "unknown"
   without rewarding it extinguishes abstention (HA3). (c) Letting "unknown" win the vote raises abstention under the
   abstention prompt (HU3 2/4, HK3 2/2) and cuts invented-answer targets in a loop round (HL1 3/3), but does not reduce
   plain-prompt confident answering by the registered margins (HU1, HK1, HL2 fail) and re-drifts by round 5 (HM2 0/3).
   (d) Weighting "unknown" x3 collapses into refusal (2/2): the reward has a sharp stability boundary.
Net: the measurement and mechanism results are strong and replicated; no registered positive-method claim survives intact.
Whether a trained arm improves the abstention-prompt selective-answering frontier (the fair method test) is registered as
A5b: FB1-FB3 FAIL (see dirA5/docs/A5_A6_A7_results_2026-09-24.md).
