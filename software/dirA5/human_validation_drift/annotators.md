# Human validation packet 2: annotators

Supplied by the lead author on 2026-09-26, after the label files were received and before the labels were joined with the
key.

| role | background | relation to the authors | pay | time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| annotator 1 | PhD student in mathematics, 4th year | external | USD 500 | about 5 h |
| annotator 2 | PhD student in mathematics, 3rd year | external | USD 500 | about 5 h |
| adjudicator | postdoctoral researcher in mathematics | external | USD 500 | about 5 h |

- None of the three had read the paper or seen model outputs before annotating.
- No LLM assistance was used during annotation.
- The annotators labelled independently, following packet.md, and were blind to round, seed and judge labels. The
  adjudicator resolved the disagreements (labels_adjudicated.csv, with reasons in adjudication_rationale.csv).
- Pay is about USD 100 per hour.
- Consent (lead author, 2026-09-26): all three agreed to the use of their labels in the paper and to their anonymised release.
- Ethics review: none (no formal board review): expert annotation of synthetic arithmetic problems, no personal data.
