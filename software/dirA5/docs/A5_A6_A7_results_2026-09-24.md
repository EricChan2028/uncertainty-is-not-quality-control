- 112 UMWP unanswerable + 112 hard-answerable items (bin-matched at base), 8 fresh plain-prompt solutions per item from base
  and own r5 s0-2 (and r3, descriptive); 896 units labelled blind by 180 Opus labellers (transcripts: each ran only its

  (gap flag per sample), 86% exact agreement on the number of readings.
- M-P1 HOLDS: at base, disagreement is reading-driven (READINGS or MIXED) in 94% of unanswerable units vs 26% of hard-answerable
  units (+0.68, CI 0.59-0.77); hard items disagree through computation errors (82/112 ERRORS). The band's agreement statistic
  cannot tell these apart, but the disagreement is of a different, interpretive kind.
- M-P3 HOLDS 3/3: readings per unanswerable item collapse from 3.87 at base to 2.83 / 3.16 / 2.65 at r5 (r3: 2.92 / 3.32 /
  2.91); hard-answerable items stay at ~1.35 (analogue 1/3).
- M-P2 FAILS - in the opposite direction: explicit gap-flagging on unanswerable items RISES from 15% at base to 62 / 49 / 70%
  at r5 (r3 56 / 46 / 65%; hard items 1% -> 3-6%). The drifted solver says "assuming ..." or "not specified" far more often,
  yet converges on one accommodation and commits to an answer (all 8 answers identical in 21 / 13 / 37% of unanswerable
  units at r5 vs 0% at base).
- Per UMWP category (unanswerable): missing key information readings 4.2 -> 2.8, gap flags 13% -> 60%; ambiguous key
  information 4.0 -> 2.9, 19% -> 69%; unrealistic conditions 2.8 -> 3.0, 8% -> 39% (small n for the other two).
- Reading: self-play does not hide the gap - it teaches the solver to NAME an assumption and settle on a default reading,
  which is how confident answering of unanswerable questions rises without loss of awareness.
- EXPLORATORY (post hoc, 03:10 25 Sep; dirA5/code/a8_silent.py): on unanswerable units, confident answering (modal numeric
  share >= 0.5) rises from 12.5% at base to 75 / 63 / 79% at r5, but units that are confident AND where no sample flags a
  gap stay at 6% -> 5 / 7 / 6%; at the sample level, answers with a stated assumption rise from 14% to 60 / 48 / 67% while
  silent answers fall from 82% to 37 / 50 / 30%. Hard answerable units stay silent (flagged 1% -> 3-6%). So the rise in
  confident answering on unanswerable questions is almost entirely explicit accommodation ("assuming X, the answer is ..."),
  not silent invention - which softens a "hallucination" reading of the harm and should be framed as learned
  presupposition accommodation.
- Reproducibility note: the regenerated answers are fresh samples from the same sampler (identical answer vectors to the
  original dumps in 16-96 of 224 items; vLLM per-request seeding is not stable across batch composition).
