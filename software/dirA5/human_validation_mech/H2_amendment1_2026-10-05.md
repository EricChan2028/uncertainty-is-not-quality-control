# H2 amendment 1: re-annotation on new items (2026-10-05, before any label of the new packets exists)

## 1. Why the amendment

The first H2 packet (registration `H2_registration_2026-10-04.md`, logged 2026-10-04 21:45:36) went to the annotators. Twelve
label files for it were received between 2026-10-04 22:20 and 2026-10-05 06:50 (SHA-256 values in section 6). None of them was
joined with the key or compared with the LLM labels. They are set aside, because they could not be established as independent
annotations of the packet: files said to come from different annotators, and files sent later as corrections, agree with one
another label for label (for example, on all 120 flags of part B). The lead author chose to repeat the annotation on items
that the first packet did not contain, with a larger sample.

## 2. What changes

- **New items** (`h21_packet.py`, `random.Random(20261006)`), excluding every item of the first packet (its 40 part-A units and
  its 120 part-B solutions):
  - Part A: all 184 remaining A8 base units, 92 unanswerable (U) and 92 hard answerable (A); the A8 set has 224 base units in
    all, so 184 is the maximum.
  - Part B: 600 single solutions: for each of the 112 U questions and of 38 A questions drawn at random, two base-model solutions
    and two round-5 solutions from two different seeds (pairs (0,1), (1,2), (2,0) in rotation), sample indices drawn at random.
    Some base solutions of part B also appear within part-A units; the annotators do part B first.
- **Two versions**: each annotator gets the same items in an independent random order under version-specific ids (A1-001..,
  B1-001.. for annotator 1; A2-001.., B2-001.. for annotator 2). Annotators work through the items in order and note the
  start and end time of each session.
- **Integrity checks** (`h21_read.py`): I1, every filled id of a file belongs to that annotator's version; I2, if the two
  annotators' label columns agree position by position (same raw id number, which maps to different items in the two versions)
  on more than 90% of the rows both filled in a part, the files are treated as copies and neither is analysed in that part.
- **Partial work**: an annotator enters a part with at least 40 valid part-A labels or 120 valid part-B flags (the sizes of the
  first packet). Items are randomly ordered, so a partial set is a random subset.
- **H2b** is computed per question from two solutions per arm (mean flag of the round-5 solutions minus that of the base
  solutions), with the bootstrap over questions.
- Return requested by 2026-10-09 20:00 CST; the inclusion cut-off stays 2026-10-10 12:00 CST.

Everything else is unchanged from `H2_registration_2026-10-04.md`: the labels and their definitions, the primary quantities
H2a, H2b and H2c, the bootstrap (4,000 replicates; seed now 20261006), and the interpretation bands of its section 6 with their
consequences for the paper.

- Part B: gap_flag rate on U 0.134 at base and 0.567 at round five (rise 0.433 over 112 questions); on A 0.013 and 0.053.
