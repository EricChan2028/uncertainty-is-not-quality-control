# H2 annotators (human check of the mechanism labels, packet of amendment 1)

Reported by the authors on 7 October 2026, after the read (h21_read.json):

- Two external annotators labelled the two differently ordered versions of the packet (ann1, ann2), independently and
  without AI assistance, blind to model, round and the LLM labels.
- Payment: USD 1,000 each.
- Total working time: 20.5 hours and 18.5 hours (per-annotator time logs were not provided).
- Consent and data: as for the H1 annotators (dirA5/human_validation_drift/annotators.md): they consented to the use and
  anonymised release of their labels; the packets contain model-written solutions and UMWP questions only, no personal data.
- The annotation was not reviewed by an ethics board.
- The label files of the first packet (registration 2026-10-04) were set aside unread (amendment 1 lists their hashes).
